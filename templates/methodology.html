1. Leakage-Free Preprocessing Pipeline

Chronological Splits & Causal Weather Alignment

Strict Out-of-Time Partitioning: Trained strictly on 2004-2017 (1,250 fires), tuned on 2018-2021 Validation (207 fires), and evaluated once on 2022-2025 Protected Test (187 fires).
Causal UTC Weather Join (floor - 1h): Local Europe/Lisbon alert timestamps are converted to UTC and matched to the most recent fully completed NASA POWER hour prior to ignition.
Training-Only Z-Score Standardization: Imputation medians, means, and standard deviations are computed strictly on the 1,250 training rows (preprocessing_training_statistics.csv).
2. Hybrid Ensemble Formulation

Overcoming Jensen's Inequality & Log-Back-Transform Bias

Why Pure Log-Target Models Underpredict Large Fires: Fitting solely to log1p(AreaTotal_ha) estimates the conditional geometric mean. By Jensen's inequality, E[expm1(Y)] > expm1(E[Y]), compressing extreme predictions below 8 ha.
80% Log-Space SVR + 20% Raw-Space Poisson HistGBR: Combining 0.80 * expm1(SVR_log) + 0.20 * Poisson_raw preserves high precision on routine small fires while capturing upper-tail drought escalation.
Why SGIF-Only (18 Features) Beats Weather-Enriched (23 Features): Local FWI sub-indices (FFMC, DMC, DC, ISI, BUI, FWI, DSR) already integrate surface weather, whereas macro-scale 0.5° × 0.625° MERRA-2 grids add collinearity.
